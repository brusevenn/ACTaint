=============Contract: GymSinglePool
Sink:
Reasoning:
- The contract has no constructor. `initialize(...)` is external and guarded only by `initializer`; `initializer` limits repeated calls but does not restrict the caller identity. It calls `__Ownable_init()` and writes privileged state such as `runnerScriptAddress`, `relationship`, `tokenAddress`, router/pair arrays, `poolInfo`, `levels`, and flags. This is a wrong-constructor/access-control sink.
- `depositFromOtherContract(...)` is external without access control and forwards an arbitrary `_from` to `_autoDeposit`. `_autoDeposit` writes `userInfo[_from]`, `user_deposits[_from]`, `totalGymnetLocked`, and possibly `totalGymnetUnlocked` without checking that `_from` is authorized or collecting tokens from `_from`. This is a sink.
- `refreshMyLevel(address _user)` is public and writes `userInfo[_user].level` for any `_user`; it does not require `_user == msg.sender` or owner authorization. This is a sink.
- `claim`, `withdraw`, `_claim`, `claimAll`, `_deposit`, and `switchToUnlocked` operate on `msg.sender` and caller-owned deposits; they do not modify other users’ records without authorization.
- `set*` functions with `onlyOwner` are access-controlled; `transferPoolRewards` is `onlyRunnerScript`; `updatePool` is permissionless by design; `safeRewardTransfer` is internal and called with `msg.sender` as recipient. These are not sinks.
