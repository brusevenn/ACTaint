Sink:
The low-level `staticcall` uses in `ownerOf` and `balanceOf` target the immutable `SEAWATER` contract and are read-only; they do not modify state or bypass access control. The constructor is the actual constructor and is not an externally callable initializer. `approve` and `setApprovalForAll` modify only the caller’s own authorization state and are guarded/standard. The access-control-sensitive operation is the transfer of position ownership in `_transfer`, because `getApproved[_tokenId]` is never cleared after transfer.
