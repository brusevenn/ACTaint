=============Contract: Cryptbond
Sink:
Reasoning: The contract has no constructor. `ToOwner` and `Mining24` are public initializer-like functions with no access control; they set `owner`, `balances`, and distribution parameters. The payable fallback mints tokens to the caller without access control or totalSupply/totalRemaining checks. `withdraw` and `withdrawForeignTokens` transfer contract assets; their `onlyOwner` guard is bypassable because `ToOwner` and `Mining24` allow any caller to overwrite `owner`.
