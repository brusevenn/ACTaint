Reviewing the contract’s state-changing functions:

- `setDrawManager` is protected by `msg.sender == creator`.
- `allocateRewardFromReserve` and `awardDraw` use `onlyDrawManager`.
- `donatePrizeTokens` and `contributeReserve` transfer tokens from `msg.sender`.
- `withdrawRewards` only modifies `_rewards[msg.sender]`.
- `claimPrize` validates the caller as the vault via `isWinner(msg.sender, ...)`.
- `contributePrizeTokens` is permissionless but only attributes already-transferred, unaccounted tokens; it does not directly transfer or withdraw assets.
- `getShutdownInfo` is `public`, has no access-control modifier, and writes `shutdownObservation` and `shutdownBalance` when shutdown is active and the observation is uninitialized. This is a one-time initializer for critical shutdown state that can be invoked by anyone.
- `withdrawShutdownBalance` and `shutdownBalanceOf` call `getShutdownInfo`, but the direct sink operation (the assignment to `shutdownObservation` and `shutdownBalance`) occurs inside `getShutdownInfo`.
