Sink:
Reasoning:
- Reviewed all external/public state-changing functions.
- Standard deposit/mint paths use `msg.sender` as the payer and only mint shares to `receiver`; they do not modify another user’s shares without authorization.
- `redeemAll` is safe because it hardcodes `owner = msg.sender`.
- `setEmergencyShutdown`, `updateTvlCap`, `removeTvlCap`, `addStrategy`, `updateStrategyAllocBPS`, `setWithdrawalQueue`, `updateWithdrawMaxLoss`, `setLockedProfitDegradation`, and `inCaseTokensGetStuck` are protected by `_atLeastRole`.
- `report` is restricted to active strategies via `strategies[stratAddr].activation != 0`.
- `revokeStrategy` allows a strategy to revoke itself or requires GUARDIAN otherwise.
- The real access-control sinks are `withdraw` and `redeem`: both allow an arbitrary `owner` and `receiver`, then `_withdraw` calls `_burn(owner, shares)` and transfers assets to `receiver` without checking `msg.sender == owner` or spending allowance.
