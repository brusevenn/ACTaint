=============Contract: ReaperVaultV2
Sink:
Reasoning:
- `withdraw` and `redeem` accept arbitrary `owner` and `receiver` arguments and route them to `_withdraw`.
- `_withdraw` executes `_burn(owner, shares)` and `IERC20Metadata(asset).safeTransfer(receiver, assets)`.
- Neither `withdraw` nor `redeem` verifies `msg.sender == owner` nor checks an allowance from `owner`. Any caller can burn another account’s shares and direct the vault assets to an arbitrary receiver.
- `redeemAll` calls `redeem(balanceOf(msg.sender), msg.sender, msg.sender)`, so it operates only on the caller’s own shares and is not an access-control sink.
- `deposit`, `depositAll`, and `mint` follow standard ERC4626 behavior: the caller supplies assets and shares are minted to the selected receiver.
- `addStrategy`, `updateStrategyAllocBPS`, `setWithdrawalQueue`, `updateWithdrawMaxLoss`, `updateTvlCap`, `removeTvlCap`, `setEmergencyShutdown`, `inCaseTokensGetStuck`, and `setLockedProfitDegradation` all enforce `_atLeastRole` with the appropriate role.
- `revokeStrategy` is restricted to the strategy itself or GUARDIAN-or-higher; `report` is restricted to active strategies.
- The constructor only initializes deployment state and grants roles at deployment.
