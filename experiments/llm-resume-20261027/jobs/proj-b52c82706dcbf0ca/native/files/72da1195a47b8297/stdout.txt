Sink:
Reasoning:
- `init` is a public initializer with no access-control or initialization guard. It writes `token`, `periods`, `interval`, and `owner`, so any caller can set or overwrite ownership and configuration. This is an access-control sink.
- Functions guarded by `onlyOwner` or `onlyHandler` are protected by `require` checks against `owner`/`handler`; they are not access-control sinks.
- `release` is permissionless, but it transfers only to the stored `schedule.beneficiary` and updates that schedule as intended; no caller-controlled recipient redirection exists, so it is not an access-control sink.
- View functions and protected `safeTransfer` calls do not introduce additional sinks.
