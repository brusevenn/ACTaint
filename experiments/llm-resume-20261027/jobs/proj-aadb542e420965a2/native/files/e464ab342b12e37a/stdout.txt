=============Contract: AutocompundStaking
Sink:
Reasoning:
- The contract has no constructor. `__AutocompundStaking_init` and `__AutocompundStaking_init_unchained` are public initializer-style functions. `onlyInitializing` does not restrict the caller, only the initialization phase. They write critical configuration state and are therefore wrong-constructor sinks.
- `compundForAccount` is public, accepts an arbitrary `account`, writes `rewards[account]`, and calls `_compound(account)`. The `updateReward(account)` modifier does not enforce caller authorization. This modifies another account’s reward/staking state without authorization.
- `_stake` and `_withdraw` are internal overrides operating on `msg.sender`; they are not externally callable in this source and do not modify other users’ assets.
- `resetLastCompound`, `setPerformanceFee`, and `setCompundReward` are protected by `onlyRole(MANAGER_ROLE)`, so they are not sinks.
- `runCompund` is permissionless and pays the compound fee to `msg.sender`; this payment is explicit contract logic. The arbitrary-account state change it invokes is captured under `compundForAccount`.
- View functions do not perform state-changing sink operations.
