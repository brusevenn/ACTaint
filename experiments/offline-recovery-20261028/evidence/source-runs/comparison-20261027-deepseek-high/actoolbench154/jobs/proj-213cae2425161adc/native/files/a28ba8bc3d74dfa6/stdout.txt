=============Contract: TokenHandler
Sink:
Reasoning:
- The contract has no constructor or initializer; no constructor-like functions are present.
- The only security-sensitive low-level operation is the assembly `call` inside `execute`. It invokes `_token` with `_data` and no ether, which can trigger arbitrary token functions from the `TokenHandler` contract’s address.
- `execute` is private, but it is called by the public functions `safeApprove`, `safeTransfer`, and `safeTransferFrom`, none of which enforce access control. Thus any external caller can make `TokenHandler` approve, transfer, or transferFrom tokens using the contract’s address and allowances.
- The public wrappers route into this single assembly call sink; they do not contain separate assembly/call sinks themselves.
