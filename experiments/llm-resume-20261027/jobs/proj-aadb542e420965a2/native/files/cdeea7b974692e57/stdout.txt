=============Contract: BaseStaking
Sink:
The standard staking, withdrawal, claim, and compound paths only modify the caller’s own balances/rewards, so they are not access-control sinks. Functions guarded by `onlyRole(...)` (`start`, `setRewardRate`, `setLimits`, `rescueTokens`) are protected. The initializer-like functions are public and have no caller authorization beyond `onlyInitializing`, which does not restrict who can call during initialization; the contract has no constructor, so these are treated as wrong-constructor sinks.

--Sink--:

If initialization is triggered, it can initialize AccessControl and execute role/configuration setup.}
