=============Contract: NFTXVaultUpgradeable
Sink:
Reasoning:
- The contract has no constructor. `__NFTXVault_init` is a public initializer.
- The `initializer` modifier only restricts repeated initialization; it does not restrict who may call it.
- `__NFTXVault_init` calls `__Ownable_init`, setting the owner to `msg.sender`, and writes critical state: `assetAddress`, `vaultFactory`, `vaultId`, `is1155`, `allowAllItems`, and all vault feature flags via `setVaultFeatures`.
- Therefore, the first caller can initialize the vault and set these critical values without authorization.
- `assignDefaultFeatures` is protected by `require(msg.sender == 0xDEA9196Dcdd2173D6E369c2AcC0faCc83fD9346a, "Not dev")`, so it is not an access-control sink.
- `setVaultFeatures`, `setManager`, `setFees`, `disableVaultFees`, `deployEligibilityStorage`, and `setVaultMetadata` are protected by `onlyPrivileged`.
- `finalizeVault` calls `setManager`, which is protected by `onlyPrivileged`.
- `mint`, `redeem`, and `swap` are standard user operations that act on the caller’s own assets or send assets to a chosen recipient; they are not access-control sinks.
- `transferERC721` and `transferFromERC721` are internal functions used within nonReentrant external flows and use the fixed `assetAddress`; they are not standalone access-control sinks.
