=============Contract: ExchangeV4
Sink:
Reasoning:
- `setMakerWallet`, `setRegistryContracts`, `pause`, `unpause`, and `withdraw` are protected by `onlyOwner`; they are not access-control sinks. The static-analysis entries for `setRegistryContracts` and `withdraw` are therefore protected by access control.
- `setRoyalty` requires the caller to be the NFT contract owner.
- `cancelBuyOrder` requires the caller to be the buyer or exchange owner and validates the buyer signature.
- `cancelPreviousSellOrders` requires the caller to be `addr` or the exchange owner.
- `fillSellOrder`: the seller signature validates the seller, but in the ERC20 payment branch the `buyer` parameter is caller-controlled and is not checked against `msg.sender` or a buyer signature. `_sendERC20PaymentsWithRoyalties` executes `safeTransferFrom(buyer, ...)` after only checking the buyer’s allowance to the exchange, so an arbitrary caller can spend an approved buyer’s ERC20 tokens.
- `fillDutchAuctionOrder`: the seller signature validates the seller, but in the ERC20 payment branch the `buyer` parameter is caller-controlled and is not checked against `msg.sender` or a buyer signature. `_sendERC20PaymentsWithRoyalties` executes `safeTransferFrom(buyer, ...)` after only checking the buyer’s allowance, so an arbitrary caller can spend an approved buyer’s ERC20 tokens.
- `fillBuyOrder`: the buyer signature validates the buyer, but the `seller` parameter is caller-controlled and is not checked against `msg.sender` or a seller signature. `_transferNFT` executes `erc721.transferFrom(seller, buyer, tokenId)` or `erc1155.safeTransferFrom(seller, buyer, tokenId, quantity, "")` after only checking the seller’s approval to the exchange, so an arbitrary caller can transfer an approved seller’s NFT to the buyer.
- Other NFT transfers in `fillSellOrder` and `fillDutchAuctionOrder` are signed by the seller; ERC20 payments in `fillBuyOrder` are signed by the buyer; ETH payments in `fillSellOrder` and `fillDutchAuctionOrder` are paid by the caller or sent to the signed seller. These are not access-control sinks.
