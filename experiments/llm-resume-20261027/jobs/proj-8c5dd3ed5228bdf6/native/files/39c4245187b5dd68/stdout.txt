=============Contract: MinterProxyV2
Sink:
Reasoning:
- `setLiquidpool`, `setWrapNative`, `pause`, `unpause`, `call`, and `withdrawFee` are protected by `onlyOwner`; they are not sinks.
- `vaultIn`, `vaultInAndCall`, `mintTo`, and the internal `_callAndTransfer` path are protected by `onlyController`; they are not sinks from an access-control perspective.
- `_getMinter`, `_isMinter`, and `isMinterWithData` use `staticcall` and are view-only; they do not modify state or enforce access control. They are not sinks.
- `vaultOut` transfers or burns only the caller’s own assets using `_msgSender()`, so it is not an access-control sink.
- `swap` is externally callable without `onlyOwner` or `onlyController`. It accepts arbitrary `target` and `callData`, grants arbitrary targets a `MAX_UINT256` allowance of `tokenAddr`, and executes external calls as `MinterProxy`. These are access-control-sensitive sink operations.
