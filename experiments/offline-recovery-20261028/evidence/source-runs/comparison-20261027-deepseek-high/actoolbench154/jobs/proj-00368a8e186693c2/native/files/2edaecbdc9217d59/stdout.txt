Reasoning:
- `constructor()` is the actual constructor; it initializes `_owner` during deployment and is not externally re-invocable after deployment.
- `renounceOwnership()` modifies `_owner` but is protected by `onlyOwner`.
- `lock()` modifies `_owner` and `_previousOwner` but is protected by `onlyOwner`.
- `unlock()` modifies `_owner` but is protected by `require(_previousOwner == msg.sender)` and the timelock condition.
- `transferOwnership()` is `public`, writes `_owner`, and has no access-control check such as `onlyOwner`. Any caller can reassign ownership.
