Sink:
The constructor only sets immutable addresses and initializes approvals at deployment time; it is not externally callable after deployment and does not introduce an access-control sink.

In `swap`, the call to `zeroXExchangeProxy.call(swapData)` is a security-sensitive low-level call. `swap` is public with no caller restriction, and `swapData` is fully caller-controlled. Since the contract has approved `underlyingToken` to `zeroXExchangeProxy` for `type(uint256).max`, arbitrary calldata can be used to invoke proxy functionality that moves the swapper contract’s approved tokens to an attacker-controlled destination. The other external calls (`bentoBox.withdraw`, `stargateRouter.instantRedeemLocal`, `bentoBox.deposit`) operate on fixed trusted contracts and the contract’s own transient balances, and are not access-control sinks in this context.
