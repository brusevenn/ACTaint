=============Contract: NovoNFT
Sink:
The unprotected `initialize` function is a sink because `initializer` only limits repeated calls, not who may call it first. The actual owner-only setters (`setCost`, `setMinStakingAmount`, `setmaxMintAmount`, `setLockDays`, etc.) and `mintForStakingV1ByOwner` are protected by `onlyOwner`, so they are not sinks despite the static-analysis entries.
