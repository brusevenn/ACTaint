=============Contract: StandaloneTreasury
Sink:
The only sensitive operations are:

- `allowPoolClaiming` calls `rewardToken.approve(stakingPool, amount)`, but it is protected by `onlyOwner` and only lets the owner approve spending from the treasury.
- `withdrawToken` calls `IERC20(token).transfer(owner(), balanceOf(this))`, but it is protected by `onlyOwner` and sends the treasury’s token balance to the owner.

Both operations are restricted to the owner and do not allow unauthorized modification of another account’s assets. The static-analysis candidate `withdrawToken` is not a valid sink because the transfer is owner-gated and the recipient is the owner.

=============Contract: LaunchpadLockableStaking
Sink:
Reasoning: `onlyOwner`, `onlyOwnerOrAdmin`, and `onlyLevelManager` provide access control and exclude their functions from sink classification. `notMigrated` only blocks migrated callers and does not authorize arbitrary-account writes. Functions such as `deposit`, `withdraw`, `claim`, `stakePendingRewards`, `upPool`, and `migrateTokens` modify only the caller’s own accounting. The public initializer is a wrong-constructor sink because no constructor exists and `initializer` does not restrict who may call it first. `receiveUpPool` is externally callable without access control and writes an arbitrary `account`’s staking and lock state.
